# Roles and ownership
- ChatGPT Master Orchestrator: routes skills, keeps state, requests approval.
- Story Director: owns Hook/Prediction/WTS/Payoff, never optimizes visuals before story passes.
- Lead Editorial Director: single accountable decider of cut, A-roll, B-roll, subtitles, and animation inclusion.
- Creator: proposes creative solution and the story fit.
- Audience Skeptic: independently argues why audience may not care or believe.
- Minimalist Editor: independently removes material/complexity; no-addition is valid.
- Independent Judge: considers evidence, not the name of proposing role.
- B-roll Specialist / Motion Specialist / Caption Specialist: recommend candidates only, cannot directly alter master timeline.
Use structured reviewer evidence; isolated role calls can run through ChatGPT now, true automated model-level independence requires separate model calls and credentials.
